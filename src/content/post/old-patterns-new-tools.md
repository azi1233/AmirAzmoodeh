I accidentally inserted a SIM ejector pin into the microphone hole and pushed it in pretty hard. During my first phone call afterwards, I realized something was wrong. My voice sounded as if it was coming from behind a wall.

Naturally, I opened ChatGPT and asked what could be wrong. It asked for my phone model and explained that many phones have a plastic acoustic mesh that might have been damaged. In the worst case, the microphone itself could be broken.

I even installed a Samsung app to test the hardware. Its microphone test went like this:

* Step 1: Record your voice.
* Step 2: Was the sound okay?

Come on, man! I'm asking YOU to test it for me!

ChatGPT gave me all kinds of scenarios, from replacing the acoustic mesh to replacing the microphone board. I was already preparing myself for a visit to the repair shop and rehearsing the possible conversations with the technician in my head.

Then I decided to watch a YouTube video about replacing the microphone. The whole phone had to be opened, with a thousand components disassembled. I thought, well, that's it. No other way.

By chance, I came across a video explaining that the microphone itself wasn't directly behind the hole. I carefully cleaned the hole with a pin, and the microphone started working again.

I often tell the guys on my team not to rely too much on AI. But this time, I fell into the same trap myself. Instead of helping me find a simple solution, AI sent me down a rabbit hole of possible hardware failures and repairs.

By the way, I couldn't find anything about acoustic mesh in phone microphones in my searches, either.

**Disclaimer:** I hope Claude fans find their tool a little more mature than this. 😏

But maybe there's a bigger lesson here.

We humans tend to repeat self-destructive patterns of behavior. We spend too much time shopping online, keep saying things to people that we later regret, say yes to tasks we don't have time for, and then procrastinate on them.

The tools change, but the patterns remain.

Using AI the wrong way might be another example: trusting it too much, accepting its answers without questioning them, and letting it reinforce our assumptions instead of helping us challenge them.

AI is a new tool, but falling into old patterns is nothing new.
